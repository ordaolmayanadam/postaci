### Yaş derecelendirmesi (IARC anketi)

- Uygulama türü: oyun değil; iletişim / verimlilik uygulaması.
- Şiddet, cinsellik, kumar, uyuşturucu ve benzeri içerik: **hayır**.
- Kullanıcılar birbiriyle iletişim kuruyor mu ya da içerik paylaşıyor mu: **evet** (e-posta). Beklenen sonuç
  "3+ / Herkes" ve "Kullanıcılar Etkileşime Girer" (Users Interact) notu.
- Konum paylaşımı, uygulama içi satın alma, reklam: **hayır**.

### Ekran görüntüleri

- En az bir, en fazla on adet; 1366×768 ya da daha büyük (önerilen 1920×1080), PNG.
- Gerçek posta, kişi adı, adres ya da sunucu adı **görünmemeli**: deneme posta kutusu ve uydurma içerik kullanın.
- Önerilen sıra: posta (okuma bölmesi açık, açık tema) → konuşma görünümü → takvim haftalık görünüm → kişiler →
  koyu tema → ayarlar. Her iki dil için ayrı görüntü yüklenebilir; arayüz dilini Ayarlar → Görünüm → Dil'den seçin.

## English (en-US)

**Product name:** Postacı

**Short description** (1000 characters)

> A fast, modern mail, calendar and contacts app for on-premises Exchange Server. Connects through Exchange Web
> Services, has no telemetry and talks only to your own server.

**Description** (10,000 characters)

> Postacı is a modern Windows client for mailboxes hosted on on-premises Exchange Server 2016, 2019 and Subscription
> Edition. Reach your mail, calendar and contacts through Exchange Web Services (EWS) in a clean interface that fits
> right into Windows.
>
> MAIL
> • New mail arrives instantly, announced by Windows notifications and a taskbar badge.
> • Conversation view, multi-select and drag-to-folder.
> • Server-side search, rich-text compose, drafts, attachments and signatures.
> • Accept, tentatively accept or decline meeting invitations right from the message.
>
> CALENDAR
> • Day and week time grids and a month agenda; drag events to reschedule.
> • Create meetings and see attendee availability.
>
> CONTACTS
> • Your personal contacts and the company directory (Global Address List) in one place.
>
> SAFE AND PRIVATE
> • Scripts are disabled in messages and remote images are blocked by default.
> • Your password is kept only in Windows Credential Manager.
> • Password-less connection with your Windows session on domain-joined PCs (Kerberos/NTLM).
> • No telemetry, ads or analytics; the app connects only to your Exchange server.
>
> MADE FOR WINDOWS
> • Light and dark themes, Mica backdrop, system tray and start with Windows.
> • English and Turkish interface; date and time formats follow the chosen language.
>
> Postacı is open source (MIT license) and was largely developed with the help of AI. Source code, issues and
> contributions: github.com/ordaolmayanadam/postaci
>
> Requires on-premises Exchange Server 2016 or later with EWS access. Exchange Online (Microsoft 365) and modern
> authentication (OAuth) are not supported.

**What's new in this version** (1500 characters)

> First release on the Microsoft Store. If you used the earlier zip version, your account settings and preferences are
> moved over automatically the first time you start the app.

**Product features** (up to 20, 200 characters each)

- Supports on-premises Exchange Server 2016, 2019 and Subscription Edition
- Instant new mail, Windows notifications and taskbar badge
- Conversation view and server-side search
- Drag-and-drop calendar grid with attendee availability
- Personal contacts and the company directory
- Password-less sign-in with your Windows session on domain-joined PCs
- Script-free reading, remote images blocked by default
- No telemetry; connects only to your own server
- Light and dark themes, English and Turkish interface

**Additional system requirements**

> On-premises Exchange Server 2016 or later with Exchange Web Services (EWS) access. On Windows 10, the Microsoft Edge
> WebView2 Runtime is required (it ships with Windows 11).

**Search terms** (up to 7)

Exchange, email, mail, calendar, EWS, Outlook alternative, contacts
